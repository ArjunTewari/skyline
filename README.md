# Skyline Exports Pvt Ltd

Editable website built with React, TypeScript and Vinext. All seven requested sections are in app/page.tsx; visual styling is in app/globals.css and page metadata in app/layout.tsx.

## Run locally
Install Node.js 22.13 or newer, then run `npm install` and `npm run dev`. Build with `npm run build`.

## Inquiry form
The form validates required fields and downloads a plain-text inquiry brief. It does not submit, transmit or persist data. To receive inquiries, connect a verified business inbox through a server-side email provider or an approved form service, then revise the preview notices and privacy information. Do not claim successful delivery until the delivery service confirms receipt.

## Before public launch
Confirm incorporation and the legal name, business email, address, privacy information, and the actual product portfolio. Replace the company-in-formation language only once accurate. Do not add certification badges, partner logos, market claims or license numbers without verification and authorization.

The website describes a prospective sourcing and export coordination business, not a manufacturer. Categories indicate areas to explore rather than approved or available products.
